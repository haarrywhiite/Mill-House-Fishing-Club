Local Festivities & Dates
  • Móra la Nova Festa Major 2026 25 Jul – 3 Aug

    25 July – 3 August 2026. The closing fireworks display is on Monday 3 August, with music, family activities, food, parades and local celebrations throughout the festival.

  • Tivissa Festa Major de Sant Jaume 2026 17–26 Jul

    Ten days of music, the traditional Dansa de Tivissa, correfoc, concerts and family events.

  • Garcia Festa Major 2026 30 Jul–16 Aug

    Local festivities with music, the Fira dels Sentits, a correfoc, family activities and the traditional river kayak descent.

  • Móra d’Ebre Festa Major 2026 17–26 Aug

    A ten-day celebration with concerts, correfoc, the Festa del Riu and a grand fireworks display.

  • Fira Agrícola, Ramadera i Industrial — Móra la Nova 22–25 Oct

    A major October fair with agriculture, livestock, machinery, trade stands and a fairground for all ages.

About Us

House notes, guest guidance and practical advice.

A little peace of heaven

Do not drink the tap water, it is OK for cooking.

We have a septic tank, so please only use the toilets for poo, pee and paper. All other items go in the bins provided in each bathroom.

No bleach products to be used in the villa.

House guidance

Please use the swimming pool towels around the pool, not the white bathroom towels.

Keep all doors and windows closed at night unless you would like the company of the local mosquitoes.

Please close all umbrellas by the pool when not in use and remove all inflatables from the pool at night.

Useful extras

We can reserve a table for you at the local restaurants.

If you need help or advice with anything, do not hesitate to call or WhatsApp us on 0034 687156179.
